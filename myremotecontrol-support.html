Troubleshooting

  • TV not found: make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, and allow Local Network access for the app in iPhone Settings. You can also add the TV by its IP address.
  • Pairing: choose "Allow" on the prompt that appears on your TV.
  • Power on doesn't work: turning the TV on over Wi-Fi depends on your TV's network wake setting, which must be enabled on the TV.
