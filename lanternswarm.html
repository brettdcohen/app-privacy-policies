Privacy Policy

Lantern Swarm — a firefly-swarm game for iPhone.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

We collect no data

Lantern Swarm does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits in the App Store version.

What stays on your device

Your fireflies, bloodline, unlocks and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Beta (TestFlight) builds

Pre-release test builds may include a feedback button. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose to send; a report then includes a screenshot, your note and the device model, and goes to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) so we can fix the problem.

Test builds also send one short performance report at the end of each night played: frame timings, the number of fireflies and moths on screen, the device model and screen refresh rate. It holds no screenshot, no name and nothing you typed, and goes to the same provider so we can keep the game smooth.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
