Privacy Policy

MyRemoteControl — an iPhone remote control for LG smart TVs.

Effective date: June 11, 2026

We collect no data

MyRemoteControl does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits.

What stays on your device

To work as a remote control, the app saves a small amount of information only on your iPhone:

  • Your TV's name, network address (IP), and hardware address (MAC), so the app can reconnect to it.
  • A pairing key issued by your TV when you approve the connection, stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove a TV from the app or delete the app.

Network use

The app communicates only with the TV you select, directly over your own local Wi-Fi network. No information is sent to us or to any external server on the internet.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
