Privacy Policy

Safekeep — a Face ID and pattern-locked private vault for photos, documents and notes on iPhone.

Effective date: October 24, 2026

We collect no data

Safekeep does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. The app never connects to the internet.

Everything stays on your iPhone, encrypted

Photos, videos, scanned documents, files and notes you put in the vault are encrypted on your iPhone with a key that is created on your iPhone and kept in its Secure Keychain. That key is protected by Face ID (or your device passcode) and by the pattern you draw. The vault is stored in a folder that is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so nothing in it ever leaves the phone unless you choose to share an item or make a backup file.

Backup files

If you create a backup, Safekeep writes a single encrypted file protected by a passphrase you choose. Where you save it (iCloud Drive, another app, a computer) is your choice; Safekeep does not send it anywhere itself. The passphrase is never stored and cannot be recovered.

Permissions

  • Face ID — to unlock the vault. Face ID data never leaves iOS; Safekeep only receives a yes or no.
  • Photos — only if you ask Safekeep to delete the originals of photos you moved into the vault. iOS asks you to confirm each time.
  • Camera — only when you scan a document. Scans go straight into the encrypted vault.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item, wiping the vault (Settings ▸ Wipe vault) or deleting the app removes the data from your iPhone. We cannot access or recover it.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
