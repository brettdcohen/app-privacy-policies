Gridlock: Traffic Control — Support

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Need help?

Email brettdavidcohen@gmail.com with your question or a description of the problem. Mentioning your device (for example "iPhone 15") and the level you were on helps. Every message is read.

How do I play?

Tap near an intersection to switch which road gets the green light. Cars find their own way; your job is timing. Switching while a car is still crossing causes a crash, and holding one direction too long backs up the queues. When the gridlock meter reaches 100 the shift is over.

What are the campaign levels?

Nine shifts, each adding one mechanic: one intersection, two, a three-junction town, build phases, pedestrians, trains, ambulances, rush surges, and a four-junction grid. Clearing the campaign unlocks Endless mode.

Where is my progress saved?

Campaign progress is saved on your device only. It is not tied to an account, and deleting the app deletes it.
