Stellar Sort — Support

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Need help?

Email brettdavidcohen@gmail.com with your question or a description of the problem. Mentioning your device (e.g. "iPhone 15") and what level you were on helps us help you faster. We read every message.

How do I play?

Tap a silo to lift its top planet, then tap another silo to drop it onto a matching color (or an empty silo). Clear a level when every silo holds a single color. Use Undo and Restart any time, or tap Hint if you're stuck.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress — levels, stars, stardust, daily streak, and owned packs — is saved on your device. It is not tied to an account, so it stays on the phone where you played.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Open the Star Map and use Backup to copy your progress code, then use Restore on the other device and paste it in. For your purchased Challenge Packs, use Restore Purchases (see below).

I bought a pack but don't see it

Open the Packs screen and tap Restore Purchases. This asks the App Store to re-confirm anything you've bought and re-unlocks it. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you purchased with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

What about stardust I bought?

Stardust is a consumable used for power-ups, so it's added to your balance at purchase and isn't part of "Restore Purchases." If a stardust purchase was charged but not delivered, email us with the date and we'll sort it out.

Refunds and billing

All purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have trouble telling the colors apart

Turn on Symbols (the 👁 button) to add a distinct shape to each color — handy for colorblind players and busy boards.

Privacy

Stellar Sort collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
