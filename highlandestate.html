Privacy Policy

Highland Estate — a calm farming game for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

If you never use Family, nothing leaves your device

Highland Estate has no account or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Your estate and settings are saved only on your device. Unless you start or join a family (below), the game sends nothing anywhere.

Family: what is stored when you start or join one

Family is optional. It lets a small group (up to six people who share a join code) ask each other for goods, send gifts, and look at each other's estates. To make that work, the following is kept on our server while you are in a family:

  • The nickname you type (up to 12 characters). It does not have to be your real name. The other members of your family see it.
  • A random member number and a random key made by the game, so the server knows which member is which. They are not tied to your name, your Apple ID, your email, or your device's identifiers. The server keeps only a scrambled form of the key.
  • A small picture of your estate: what is built and planted and where. It never includes your coins, your stores, your orders or your saved game. The other members of your family can see it when they visit.
  • Your requests and gifts: which goods, how many, between which members, and whether they were taken.

The game does not ask for and does not send your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier.

What it is used for

Only to show your family's members, requests, gifts and estates to the people in that family. It is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking, and it is never sold or shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • Leave the family (the "Leave this family" button on the Family screen) and your nickname, your estate picture and your open request are deleted from the server at once. The same happens if the person who started the family removes you.
  • After you leave, a nameless record (your random member number and the gifts that passed through the family) stays for as long as the family exists, so that every member's game can still learn how its gifts ended.
  • When the last member leaves, or when nobody in the family has opened the game for 60 days, the whole family and everything in it is deleted.

Network addresses

The server is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any internet service, it sees the network (IP) address a request comes from. We use it only to limit wrong join codes and the number of new families, keeping a scrambled form for a few days at most, never the address itself. Cloudflare processes addresses and short technical logs to deliver the service; those logs contain no nickname, join code or key.

Bug reports in test builds

Test builds include a "BUG" button. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose to send a report. A report contains a screenshot of the game, the note you type, your device model and iOS version, and a short log of recent game events. It is sent to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) and used only to fix the game. The App Store version does not include this button.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Family is meant for people who know each other and share a join code in person; there is no chat, no public matching and no way to find or contact strangers. A parent who wants a child's family data removed can have the child leave the family in the game, or can email us.
