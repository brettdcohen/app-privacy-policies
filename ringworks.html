Privacy Policy

Ringworks — a calm factory-building game for iPhone.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

We collect no data

Ringworks does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

Everything stays on your iPhone

Your progress (factories, planets and the ring) and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Bug reports in test builds

Test builds include a "BUG" button. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose to send a report. A report contains a screenshot of the game, the note you type, your device model and iOS version, and a short log of recent game events. It is sent to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) and used only to fix the game. The App Store version does not include this button.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
