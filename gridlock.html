Privacy Policy

Gridlock: Traffic Control — a traffic-light puzzle game for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

We collect no data

Gridlock does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The game contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, and no third-party software development kits.

What stays on your device

The game saves your campaign progress and which tips you have already seen only on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

Gridlock works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
