Stellar Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

The short version

Stellar Sort does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It is a single-player puzzle game with no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you need to know fits on this page.

What we store, and where

Your game progress — completed levels, star ratings, stardust balance, daily-streak count, and which Challenge Packs you own — is saved only on your own device, inside the app's local storage. It never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or to any server. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

In-app purchases

Stellar Sort offers optional in-app purchases (Challenge Packs and stardust). All payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, payment-card details, or any other billing information. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. To restore previously purchased packs, the app asks Apple to confirm your past purchases; this is handled by Apple's StoreKit and involves no data collection by us.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

Stellar Sort is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We do not knowingly gather data from children or anyone else.

Data sharing

We have no personal data to share, sell, or disclose — so we never do.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
