Privacy Policy

Facet — a daily 3D-reasoning trainer for iPhone.

Effective date: October 24, 2026

We collect no data

Facet does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits in the App Store version.

What stays on your device

Your practice history, drill levels, Checkpoint scores and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Reminders

If you turn on the optional practice reminder, the notification is scheduled locally by iOS on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Beta (TestFlight) builds

Pre-release test builds may include a feedback button. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose to send; a report then includes a screenshot, your note and the device model, and goes to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) so we can fix the problem.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
